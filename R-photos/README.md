# R-photos

This folder contains real photographs of the **Mitsos Open-Source AI Humanoid Robot**.  
The images document the assembled robot from different angles and highlight the mechanical design, electronics integration, and overall appearance.

> **Note:** The robot shown in these photos is **not the final version**. Future revisions will include improved cable management, full camera ring light integration, redesigned ToF sensor mounts, and additional mechanical refinements.
